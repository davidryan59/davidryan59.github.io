Every part of this piece, artwork and traits alike, is read straight out of the contract on Optimism. Nothing is stored on a server.

I built Moving Mondrian for the Optimism Foundation's "We Love The Art" competition in January 2024. The artwork and every one of its traits come out of the contract itself. Rarity, shape, frame, lines, base angle, opacities and colours are all generated onchain, so each piece lives entirely on Optimism mainnet with no server behind it. Proceeds from this mint are split evenly between the artist and Protocol Guild, to support Ethereum core development.
