# Leech explorer video: storyboard

A 42-second 4:3 video of the Leech Lattice Explorer at drbuild.uk/leech,
version 1. It plays as a seamless loop: it starts and ends on the 36-fold
view in Shapes colour, with the title showing and the pointer on the
36-fold button. There is no voice, music or end card. `timeline.js` holds
every number here and is the source of truth. David approved the plan on
2026-10-19.

The story moves between the rotation scenes, which have the most spots,
through scenes with few spots: the Hexagon, Coordinates and E8 inside
Leech. Rings colour, set on 60-fold, travels with the balls into the next
scenes. Shells shows E8's 240 roots in gold. Layers colours the balls by
their angle from one ball.

## Format

- 1440 × 1080, 30 fps, 1,260 frames, silent. Times are plain seconds.
- The app's own page in its dark theme, filmed whole at 1280 × 960 CSS px,
  with the details panel collapsed to its rail.
- A real mouse pointer makes every click. Pointer moves follow half a sine
  wave. Scene changes take 2 s, and Next E8 its fixed 4 s.
- Something moves from 0.25 s. From 40.4 s, a still of the first frame
  fades in, and nothing moves from 41.2 s to the end.
