Question 2: tumbling and closing

A square tumbling in a circle. A tumbling shape skips part of the rim, so a simple size ratio no longer gives a short curve. Clean rolling at 30 in 70 would close after 3 laps. A small size change brings the tumbling square back to a short curve.
