# Unimodular lattices video, 60-second storyboard

## Summary

The 60-second version keeps the arc of the 30-second film: ℤⁿ, E₈, dimension
16, the Niemeier lattices, Leech and the Monster. It adds five beats: E₈ built
from two halves, the 24 lattices merging into one, the explosion in the
count, the j-function on the hyperbolic disc, and the Monster fusing three
pieces into one. Each caption stays up for 4 to 6.5 seconds.

## Format

- 1080 × 1080, 60 fps, exactly 3600 frames, silent, captions in the picture.
- The dark builder-page theme, STIX Two captions at the top, and bright
  points that glow.
- The last frame flows into the first.

## Beats

| Time | Picture | Caption |
|---|---|---|
| 0–5 s | The integer grid on a tilted plane, receding into soft focus. The camera pushes in, and one gold unit cell pulses. | **Unimodular lattices.** Whole-number dot products, one point per unit of volume. Up to dimension 7, the only one is ℤⁿ |
| 5–10 s | Grid points lift off with motion trails. They tumble as an E₈ cloud in 3D, then snap flat into the 30-fold Coxeter rosette with a shockwave. The 6,720 edges draw on as filaments. | **Dimension 8: E₈.** 240 spheres touch each one: the densest packing in 8 dimensions (Viazovska, 2016) |
| 10–15 s | The rosette rebuilds in two halves. The 112 integer roots and an 8-point core appear in gold. Then the 128 half-integer roots fall into the gaps in sky blue. A counter lands on 120 + 128 = 248. | **120 + 128 = 248.** The half-integer roots fill the deep holes, and together they make the 248 dimensions of the E₈ Lie algebra |
| 15–20.5 s | E₈ splits into E₈ ⊕ E₈ on the left as D₁₆ forms on the right. Identical sound rings pulse out of both in step, and both shell counts tick up together. | **Dimension 16: two lattices, one sound.** Different shapes, identical shell counts: their tori ring alike (Milnor, 1964) |
| 20.5–25 s | The 24 Niemeier root systems flip in as pills. | **Dimension 24: exactly 24 lattices.** Niemeier, 1973. Twenty-three are built from root systems, and one has none |
| 25–29 s | The pills slide together and fuse with a flash into a single pill, II₂₅,₁. | **Add one hyperbolic plane, and all 24 become one.** N ⊕ U ≅ II₂₅,₁ for every one of them |
| 29–33 s | The pill bursts into a starfield of thousands of particles. A rolling counter runs 1, 2, 24, then over 1,000,000,000 against dimensions 8, 16, 24 and 32. | **Then the count explodes.** Even lattices: 1, 2 and 24 in dimensions 8, 16 and 24, then over a billion in dimension 32 |
| 33–39.5 s | The starfield implodes into the Leech mandala. Its plane turns, and the picture changes from quasicrystal to flower. | **Back in dimension 24: the Leech lattice.** The one with no roots. 196,560 spheres touch each one: the densest packing in 24 dimensions |
| 39.5–45.5 s | 196,560 + 324 = 196,884 assembles in large type, and 196,884 lands with a shockwave. Below it, j(τ) = q⁻¹ + 744 + 196,884q + …, and the j-function blooms on the hyperbolic disc in domain colouring and flows along the modular group. | **Add 324, and the j-function appears.** 196,884 is its first coefficient. The modular group leaves j unchanged, and it tiles the disc below |
| 45.5–50.5 s | Three particle streams orbit apart, labelled 299, 98,280 and 98,304. They braid and fuse into one ring of 196,883, while one point, labelled 1, stays still at the centre. | **The Monster fuses three pieces into one.** A triality mixes ordinary and twisted string states into 196,883 dimensions (Frenkel, Lepowsky and Meurman, 1988) |
| 50.5–54.5 s | The ring splits into rings of 47, 59 and 71 with their star polygons, around 196,883. | **196,884 = 1 + 196,883.** 196,883 = 47 × 59 × 71. Monstrous moonshine: Conway and Norton, 1979, proved by Borcherds, 1992 |
| 54.5–59 s | The primes up to 71 appear on an outer arc. The 15 Monster primes light up in gold, and 37, 43, 53, 61 and 67 stay dim. | **The same 15 primes, twice.** They divide the Monster's order, and they are exactly the primes p where X₀(p)⁺ has genus zero. Ogg offered a bottle of Jack Daniel's for the reason |
| 59–60 s | A crossfade back to the opening grid | — |

## Effects

- Glow on every point, as in the 30-second film.
- Motion trails on fast moves, from sub-frame samples.
- A shockwave ring when a shape locks into symmetry or a key number lands.
- Rolling-digit counters, and terms that slide into place.
- Depth of field on the tilted grid and the 3D E₈ cloud.
- Match cuts by shape, rosette to rosette and ring to ring, in place of plain
  fades.
- The j-function coloured on the Poincaré disc. Every pixel's τ is reduced
  to the fundamental domain, so the q-series converges fast. The flow
  τ → τ + t repeats after t = 1, which follows Shonkwiler's looping animations
  with the modular flow (arXiv 2603.02088, 2026).
