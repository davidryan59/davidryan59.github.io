# Pentrys film storyboard

## Summary

This is the running order of the 30-second film. Times are the film's seconds. Each
wipe covers the screen at the time shown, and the sections change under it.

| Time | Picture | Main caption | Small caption |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0–1.9 | Title card, as the share card. Pieces fall away | Falling pieces of one to five squares | Try it yourself at drbuild.uk/pentrys |
| 1.9–7.2 | Natural play by the simulated player, seed 8329 | 21 shapes of one to five squares | Move, rotate, flip and drop. |
| 7.2–11.2 | A 2 clears a row with a gap, then a 3 with two | 2s and 3s pay for gaps | A 2 forgives one gap. A 3 forgives two. |
| 11.2–13.9 | A hard drop smashes two panes, for a Double | Glass counts for nothing | A hard drop smashes straight through it. |
| 13.9–18 | A flood makes a Double, then a deluge a Quad | Floods fill the gaps | A deluge reaches every hole below it. |
| 18–22 | A bomb, a row bomb and a second bomb | Bombs blast a crater | A row bomb clears its whole row. |
| 22–28 | A Pentrys, then a Hextrys with an all clear | Five rows make a Pentrys | Six rows make a Hextrys. |
| 28–30 | The pieces land and the title slams in. Still from 29.5 | As the first row | As the first row |

The last frames match the first frame, so the film loops.

## Odds

The natural play section deals 2s at 1 in 30; 3s, glass, floods and bombs at 1 in 50;
deluges and row bombs at 1 in 100. The scripted sections show fewer. `render.js --log`
prints the count for the whole film.
