Ethereum Merge Fractals

A generative collection marking Ethereum's Merge, on Ethereum mainnet.

I made these to mark Ethereum's move to Proof of Stake in September 2022. The collection runs to 5,875 pieces, each one drawn by the contract itself and named after a core protocol developer. Proceeds from every mint go straight to the developers who built the Merge, through Protocol Guild and StatefulWorks. The contract forwards the money on mint, so it never sits with me.

    How the price works

    The mint price started at 0.001 ETH and rises by 0.0002 ETH after every 50 pieces. The contract asks for the exact amount, to the wei, so this page reads the current price the moment you press mint rather than trusting a figure that may be minutes old. If a piece sells while you are confirming, and that piece happens to cross a 50 boundary, your transaction is rejected and nothing is spent beyond gas. Press mint again and the new price is used.
