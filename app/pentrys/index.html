Tutorial

Thirteen basics, then eight advanced lessons. Play them in any order.

    How to play

    Move, rotate and flip the falling pieces to fill rows. A full row clears. Cycle the queue to choose which piece falls next.

    Clear more rows at once for far more: 100, 300, 700, 1,300, 2,300 and 7,100 points for one to six rows, and half as much again for each row past six.

    Bonuses multiply together. Spare, the squares a row has over its width, is ×1.2 to ×1.8. Each glass cleared is ×1.5, and glass in a row with spare adds ×2. A streak of three or more clears in a row starts at ×1.2, and an all clear is ×2. Smashing, blasting and filling score too. Then the level multiplies it all. Drops score without it: 1 a row for a soft drop, 2 for a hard drop.
