Method and credits

Summary

The explorer constructs the 196,560 shortest vectors of the Leech lattice from the extended binary Golay code. Every picture is computed from those coordinates.

Sources

John Leech described the lattice in “Notes on sphere packings”, Canadian Journal of Mathematics 19 (1967), 251–267.

The coordinate construction follows Conway and Sloane, Sphere Packings, Lattices and Groups, chapter 4, section 11.

The E8 viewing planes and direct manipulation build on this site's E8 explorer, which credits David A. Madore, David A. Richter and Pierre-Philippe Dechant.

Each rotation scene shows a plane that an element of Co0, the lattice's symmetry group, turns. The conjugacy classes come from the ATLAS of Finite Groups, through Thomas Breuer's GAP character table library.

Rendering

The octad window groups equal restrictions and draws 2,641 Canvas spots. Dense 24-dimensional views use a two-pass WebGL 2 count buffer with RGBA8 textures.
