How to explore

Choose among E8 views, 24-dimensional rotations and other views. The number on each button counts its spots. Tap a group heading to open or close it.

Drift turns the view through hidden dimensions, so it leaves the scene. Spin turns the picture in its own plane, and Step turns it by exactly one symmetry: every spot lands on a spot.

Select a ball to inspect it. Shift-drag pans when zoomed, and H hides every control. Drag or press the Details edge to resize or hide this panel.

Rings colours each ball by its distance from the centre at the moment you choose it. The balls keep that colour as they move. Raw shows every occupied pixel at full colour. Density reveals piles and marks its glow scenes with ◐.
