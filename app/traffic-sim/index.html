    About Traffic Simulator

    Watch small speed changes grow into a jam on a ring road. Selfish and Coordinated cars use driving rules set by hand.

    Set Self-driving to 100%, then raise Selfish. Watch the speed spread as the mix changes.

    Choose the road, density, driver mix and simulation speed. Colour the cars by driver or speed, or ride along with a car.

    Drag to move, scroll or pinch to zoom, and right-drag or twist to turn. Tap a car to brake it. Space pauses and a full stop advances one step.

    The address bar keeps the simulation settings and seed. Copy the address to share that setup.

    Models and data
