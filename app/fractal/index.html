    How to edit

    Parents: the bin beside the parent numbers removes the selected parent. Children that used it switch to their own parent, and Shift-click removes those children instead.

    Children: + adds one, a grid cell in size, in the first free cell from the bottom left. The bin removes the selected child, and Shift-click removes them all. The padlock in the Children list keeps a child out of Randomise. Drag a child to move it. On a square, the circular arrow above the top edge turns it about its centre. A corner drag moves that corner and holds the opposite one; Shift keeps the shape, and Alt scales about the centre. An edge drag across the edge stretches, along it skews, and Shift does both. On a triangle, the circular arrow below the bottom edge turns it. A corner drag holds the other two corners: across the opposite edge it stretches, along it skews, and Shift frees the corner. An edge drag resizes the triangle about the opposite corner, and Alt resizes about the centre.

    Options: Frame draws every parent and child as a square or a triangle, and keeps the fractal the same. The editor always shows the grid: Cols by Rows on a square, Per side on a triangle. Snap corners snaps corners to it. Shape draws each copy as its parent's hull or as its frame, Numbers shows or hides each copy's parent number, and Style sets how copies are filled: Solid, Blend, Auto or Outline. A saved fractal keeps all of these and the calculation limits. Copy and Paste move the limits between fractals.

    Editor view: drag empty space to pan, and use the wheel or a pinch to zoom. Fit shows every child again. Drag the line under the editor to change its height.

    Keys: 1–9 and 0 pick a child's parent, and Shift with a number shows that parent. N adds a child, R and T rotate, H and V flip, Backspace removes, Shift+Backspace removes every child of the parent, ⌘D or Ctrl+D duplicates, Esc deselects.
