“Nopert” means non-Rupert. C11 is the Undecanope, an 88-vertex Nopert polyhedron (paper). There are also the 90-vertex C15 Noperthedron, the 60-vertex rhombicosidodecahedron and the 8-vertex stellated tetrahedron P11/20. The pass ratio is the largest scale at which the copy's shadow fits inside the hole.
